How We Work

From First Call to Final Placement

Five steps stand between your idea and furniture that fits your space exactly.

  1. Step 01

    Consultation

    We start with a conversation — in person, by call or on WhatsApp — to understand your space, style and budget.

  2. Step 02

    Measurements

    Our team visits your home, hotel or office to take precise measurements so every piece fits the first time.

  3. Step 03

    Workshop Craftsmanship

    Our carpenters hand-build your piece from kiln-dried hardwood, using traditional joinery and checking quality at every stage.

  4. Step 04

    Quality Inspection

    Every joint, surface and finish is checked against our quality list before a piece ever leaves the workshop.

  5. Step 05

    Delivery & Installation

    We deliver and install on-site, nationwide, and walk you through care instructions before we leave.
